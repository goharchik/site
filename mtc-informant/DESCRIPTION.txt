MTC Informant 1.1.0

A passenger information system for MTC trains. Take the "MTC Informant" item, pick a section and an announcement, and it plays in the car closest to you (up to 15 blocks). You can add your own sounds, sections and whole lines as separate add-ons.

Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.26+ and MTC.
