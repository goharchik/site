MTC Informant 1.2.0

A passenger information system for MTC trains. Take the "MTC Informant" item, pick a section and an announcement, and it plays across the whole train closest to you (up to 15 blocks). You can add your own sounds, sections and whole lines as separate add-ons.

Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.26+ and MTC.
